Review the idea, then the executable plan

Stopping rules are not correctness certificates

The upstream loop assesses cleanliness before that round’s fixes: no unsettled Blocker/Major plus adequate reviewer completion. Two consecutive clean rounds converge; the default cap is five. A failed reviewer gets one retry, then “not run,” never zero findings. Some outer stage re-audits have a separate one-re-audit cap.

Upstream says not to reopen a settled contest or accepted risk at the same scope; it states no contrary-evidence exception. Reopening on new contrary evidence is Ultimate’s local policy, not an upstream guarantee. See pinned loop and report §3.8.

A fresh transcript is not a sandbox

Seven lenses become four profiles—not equal sampling

Outside that mapping: upstream’s synthesizer and bootstrap orchestrator are not two more reviewer lenses. The coordinator absorbs synthesis; bootstrap contracts (Peanuts/Hay) and systematic drift reporting are deferred. That loses real help for underdocumented repositories.

The trade-offs, without advantage badges

Evidence stops before a usefulness claim

Related research is mixed—and is not an EGM evaluation

Self-Refine supports feedback-and-revision on selected tasks; intrinsic self-correction research warns repetition without external evidence can fail. Correlated Errors cautions against treating fresh roles or model families as independent samples.

Cross-Context Review’s updated result favors fresh review over repeated same-session review, but not significantly over single-review or context-aware-subagent conditions on average. Synthetic errors, one model, unverified outputs, a language confound and heuristic matching limit transfer.

Stephens’s replay motivates controlling freshness separately from family and auditing declined true findings. One pipeline, selected historical defects, limited sampling, grading and skill-version confounds remain. Neither paper proves this port works. Access depth and caveats: external ledger E5–E12.

Spend readers where uncertainty can change the work

The minimum useful experiment

Retain a bounded experiment, not a workflow engine or a superiority claim.

First verify actual discovery, effective configuration, resource reads and the capability decision. Then try one feature and a zero-reader control before broader adoption.

  1. Compare three arms: ordinary Ultimate; pinned EGM with a named preset; the strengthened adaptation.
  2. Match the ground truth: same request, source, accessible evidence and feasible total model/tool budget. Match model configuration in the primary comparison; test family changes separately.
  3. Judge the whole chain: valid serious catches, false positives, declined true findings, evidence dispositions, repair success, regressions and escaped defects.
  4. Count the bill: total time, tokens, retries, decision turns and implementation rework—not just reviewer count. Blind adjudication where practicable; report uncertainty and per-class results.

Conditional recommendation: test the local final-brief/lifecycle bet on authorized, coupled work. Prefer upstream’s broader documentation and restrictive-adapter strengths where those matter. For a clear correction, ordinary inspection and the relevant test may be enough.
