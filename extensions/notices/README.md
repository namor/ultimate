Notices as a stack of small cards centred under the conversation header, so they never cover the sidebar, an open right panel or the header: what the server reports with `notice` events (a failed conversation refresh, an unknown document) and what the browser reports itself (a failed command, unsaved UI state, a page that opened before every extension loaded). Info fades after 5 s, warnings after 9 s, errors after 15 s; hovering or focusing the stack keeps them, and each can be dismissed.

An `overlay` slot component over `useNotices()`. Newest on top; three show, the rest fold into "+N more", and Clear all appears once two or more are open. On a phone the stack spans the width with a small margin. Other extensions report through `store.notify` rather than drawing their own toast, so nothing lands on top of the stack.
